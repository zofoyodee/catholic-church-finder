Connect Your Stats

This page reads the counts from your free GoatCounter account. Do this once on each computer or phone you want to see stats on.

  1. Sign in at GoatCounter.
  2. Open Settings, then API, and choose New API Key.
  3. Give it any name, tick only Read statistics, and save.
  4. Copy the key and paste it below.

The key is kept only in this browser. It is never put in the website's files, so nobody else who finds this page can see your numbers.
